Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Spraylite is a fictional brand. This website is a frontend demo, so this page explains what the demo actually does with your data.

What we store

Your cart and wishlist are saved in your own browser using localStorage, so they are still there when you return. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

  • Cart: the product ids and quantities you add.
  • Wishlist: the product ids you save.

What we do not collect

The site has no accounts, payments, analytics or advertising trackers. The newsletter form only checks that an email address looks valid; the address is not sent or stored anywhere.

Third-party services

None. Fonts, images and scripts are all served from this website, so browsing the shop makes no requests to other companies. The social icons in the footer link to other websites only when you choose to open them.

Clearing your data

Empty your cart and wishlist from their drawers, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
