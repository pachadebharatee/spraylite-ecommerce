Why Spraylite

Luxury you can wear every day

Spraylite began in a small studio with one idea: fine fragrance shouldn’t be saved for special occasions. We blend in small batches, source thoughtfully and price honestly — so the scent you love can be part of every day.

  • Premium ingredients

    Natural essences and fine aroma materials, sourced from trusted growers and perfumery houses.

  • Long-lasting fragrances

    High perfume concentrations designed to stay with you from morning coffee to late dinner.

  • Elegant packaging

    Weighted glass, metal caps and recyclable boxes worth keeping on your dresser.

  • Affordable luxury

    Niche-quality perfume without the niche price tag — because luxury should be lived in.

The Spraylite journal

Made slowly, worn daily

Every Spraylite scent is blended by hand, then rested for six weeks so its notes can settle before bottling. We bottle at eau de parfum strength, so a few sprays carry you from morning into night, and each fragrance is built to feel like yours, not like everyone else's.
